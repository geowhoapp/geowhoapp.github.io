Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026 · Applies to the GeoWho iOS app

GeoWho (“we”, “the app”) is a history guessing game for iPhone. This page explains what information is involved when you use the app. We do not sell your personal data.

1. What the app stores on your device

Game progress such as unlocked figures, streaks, settings (for example Hard Mode), and related preferences are stored locally on your device via Apple’s secure app storage (Capacitor Preferences). This data stays on your device unless you delete the app or clear it.

2. Purchases & subscriptions (Apple + RevenueCat)

If you buy GeoWho Pro (subscription or lifetime), payment is processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether your purchase entitles you to Pro features (entitlement status). RevenueCat and Apple may process purchase-related identifiers according to their own privacy policies. We do not receive your full payment card details.

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy

3. Game Center (optional)

If you sign in to Apple Game Center, Apple may process your Game Center identity and scores (for example streak leaderboard). That is controlled by Apple; you can stay signed out and still play.

4. Analytics & advertising

The current version does not include third-party advertising SDKs or a separate product-analytics platform beyond what Apple and RevenueCat provide for purchases/store operations.

5. Children

GeoWho is a general-audience history game. It is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the app if you are under the age required by local law for this type of service.

6. Your choices

  • Delete local game data by removing the app or clearing app data on the device.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • Restore purchases in the app if you reinstall or change devices.
  • Sign out of Game Center in system settings if you prefer not to use leaderboards.
