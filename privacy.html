Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026 · Applies to the GeoWho iOS app and this website

GeoWho is a history guessing game for iPhone. This notice informs you about personal data processing under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), especially Articles 12–14. We do not sell your personal data.

See also the Impressum. For day-to-day support, prefer the Support form.

2. What the app stores on your device

Game progress such as unlocked figures, streaks, settings (for example Hard Mode), and related preferences are stored locally on your device via Apple’s secure app storage (Capacitor Preferences). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the app service) and Art. 6(1)(f) (legitimate interest in working offline progress). This data stays on your device unless you delete the app or clear it.

3. Purchases & subscriptions (Apple + RevenueCat)

If you buy GeoWho Pro (subscription or lifetime), payment is processed by Apple as merchant of record. We use RevenueCat to check whether your purchase entitles you to Pro features (entitlement status). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract / purchase). We do not receive your full payment card details.

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy

4. Game Center (optional)

If you sign in to Apple Game Center, Apple may process your Game Center identity and scores (for example streak leaderboard). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) / your choice to use Game Center. You can stay signed out and still play.

5. This website (GitHub Pages)

Visiting geowhoapp.github.io may create standard server/access logs at the host (GitHub / GitHub Pages), typically including IP address, user agent, and request time. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in operating and securing the site). See GitHub’s privacy statement.

If you use the Support contact form, we process your name, email address, and message to reply to you (Art. 6(1)(b)/(f) GDPR). Delivery uses a form relay (Web3Forms). Spam reduction uses a honeypot, a short timing check, and hCaptcha (bot check; may set cookies / process a challenge token). We do not use the form for marketing lists. See Support · hCaptcha Privacy.

6. Recipients & transfers outside the EU/EEA

Apple, RevenueCat, Web3Forms, and hCaptcha may process data in the United States or other countries. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards (for example EU Standard Contractual Clauses) as described in their privacy documentation. GitHub may also process website logs outside the EU/EEA.

7. Retention

  • Local game data: until you delete the app or clear app data.
  • Purchase / entitlement records: retained by Apple and RevenueCat as needed for billing, fraud prevention, and restoring purchases (see their policies).
  • Website logs: according to the host’s retention practices.

8. Your rights (GDPR)

You may have the right to:

  • access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing;
  • data portability where applicable;
  • object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f);
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Germany typically your state data protection authority; overview: BfDI).

For privacy requests, use the Support contact form or the controller contact details in section 1 / the Impressum.

9. Analytics & advertising

The current app version does not include third-party advertising SDKs or a separate product-analytics platform beyond what Apple and RevenueCat provide for purchases/store operations. Store-level metrics in App Store Connect are aggregated by Apple.

10. Automated decisions

We do not use personal data for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects (GDPR Art. 22).

11. Children

GeoWho is a general-audience history game. It is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the app if you are under the age required by local law for this type of service.

12. Your choices in the app

  • Delete local game data by removing the app or clearing app data on the device.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • Restore purchases in the app if you reinstall or change devices.
  • Sign out of Game Center in system settings if you prefer not to use leaderboards.
