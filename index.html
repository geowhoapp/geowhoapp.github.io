How it works

  1. See the map. Two markers: where they were born, where they died.
  2. Use hints. Era, category, then a portrait — each costs points.
  3. Guess the name. Fuzzy search forgives typos. Build a streak.

Free & Pro

Classic mode is free to start. GeoWho Pro unlocks Survival and the full Pro set (subscription or lifetime). Manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple ID → Subscriptions.
