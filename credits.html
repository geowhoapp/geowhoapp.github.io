Sources & Licenses

Last updated: 19 August 2026 · GeoWho iOS app

GeoWho is a history guessing game. Structured biography facts come from open databases. Portraits are Wikimedia Commons files licensed individually; the app shows author and license in Collection and on reveal screens, and links here for the full overview.

Biographies & structured data

  • Wikidata — names, birth/death places and years, coordinates, occupations (typically CC0).
  • Wikipedia — optional article links only. Wikipedia article text is CC BY-SA and is not copied into the game; hints are original GeoWho text.

Portraits

Each portrait is a file from Wikimedia Commons (via Wikidata property P18 where available). Licenses are mostly Public Domain / CC0 or Creative Commons (CC BY / CC BY-SA). Attribution appears in the app next to the portrait where it is examined (Collection, reveal, enlarged hint view). A small number of figures have no cleared portrait and are shown without an image.

Wikimedia Terms: foundation.wikimedia.org

Difficulty ranking

Uses Pantheon Historical Popularity Index (HPI) data and Wikidata sitelinks. Pantheon datasets are typically offered under Creative Commons Attribution terms — see the Pantheon project.

Maps

  • Natural Earth — land outlines (public domain).
  • OpenStreetMap — when OSM-based basemaps are shown: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Styles/tiles may include OpenFreeMap / OpenMapTiles.

Fonts

Outfit and Lora via Google Fonts — SIL Open Font License 1.1.

UI icons

Era and field icons in the game HUD are from Lucide (ISC license; commercially free). The compass icon is derived from Feather (MIT).
