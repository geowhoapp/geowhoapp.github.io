Sources & Licenses

Last updated: 19 August 2026 · GeoWho

GeoWho uses openly licensed historical data and media. Per-figure portrait credits (author and license) appear in the app where a portrait is shown.

Historical data

  • Wikidata — structured facts such as names, places, and dates (typically CC0).
  • Wikipedia — optional links to articles only. Article prose is not copied into the game.

Portraits

Portraits come from Wikimedia Commons under each file’s own license (commonly Public Domain / CC0 or Creative Commons with attribution). Some figures are shown without a portrait when a suitable license cannot be confirmed.

Wikimedia Terms of Use

Difficulty ranking

Popularity / difficulty signals use Pantheon Historical Popularity Index (HPI) data (typically Creative Commons Attribution) and Wikidata sitelink counts.

Maps

  • Natural Earth — land outlines (public domain).
  • OpenStreetMap — when map data derived from OSM is shown: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).

Typefaces

Outfit and Lora — SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Icons

Selected UI icons from Lucide (ISC) and Feather (MIT).

Other software

The app also incorporates third-party open-source components under common permissive licenses (for example MIT, Apache-2.0, or BSD). Those notices are retained with the software; this page focuses on content sources players care about.

Contact support if you believe a credit is missing or incorrect.
