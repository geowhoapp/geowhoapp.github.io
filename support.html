Subscriptions & restore

  • Buy or start a trial from the in-app Paywall (Pro).
  • Cancel or change plans: iPhone Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions.
  • Reinstalled the app? Use Restore Purchases in Settings.
  • Billing issues with a charge: also check Apple Support / purchase history — Apple handles payment.

Gameplay

  • Survival: Pro mode with lives.
  • Hard Mode: hides extra map labels / portrait path depending on settings.
